About Government Engineering College, Bhojpur

Government Engineering College, Bhojpur, is a government engineering institution established in 2019 by the Government of Bihar under the Department of Science and Technology, now known as the Department of Science, Technology and Technical Education.

Located in Arrah, Bhojpur, near Maharaja College and South-East Ramna Maidan, the institution was inaugurated by the Hon'ble Chief Minister of Bihar, Shri Nitish Kumar, under the Saat Nischay Yojana of the Bihar Government.

The college began its academic journey in 2019 at its mentor institution, Bakhtiyarpur Engineering College, Bakhtiyarpur. Since October 2023, the institution has been operating from its permanent campus.

GEC Bhojpur is affiliated with Bihar Engineering University, Patna, and offers engineering programmes approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi.

The institution was established with the objective of addressing the growing demand for technically qualified professionals and strengthening access to quality engineering education in Bihar.

Infrastructure and facilities

GEC Bhojpur provides facilities designed to support academic learning, practical training and student development. These include:

  1. High-speed Wi-Fi connectivity.
  2. 24-hour electricity supply with generator backup.
  3. Campus security facilities.
  4. Modern library with reading room.
  5. Computer centre.
  6. Auditorium.
  7. Fully equipped mechanical workshop.
  8. Modern engineering laboratories.
  9. Separate hostels for male and female students.

Institutional objective

The institution aims to combine theoretical knowledge with practical learning, technical exposure and research-oriented education.

Its focus is to prepare students for professional careers, higher studies, innovation and entrepreneurship while encouraging ethical values and social responsibility.
