About the college

Government Engineering College, Bhojpur was established in 2019 by the Government of Bihar under the Department of Science and Technology, now the Department of Science, Technology and Technical Education. It stands in Arrah, near Maharaja College and South-East Ramna Maidan.

The college was inaugurated by Chief Minister Nitish Kumar under the Saat Nishchay Yojna. It has operated from its permanent campus since October 2023, with the aim of meeting the growing technical needs of society.
