Admissions

Admission to B.Tech is through BCECE counselling. Confirm all dates on the official notices.

  1. Qualify in BCECE
    Appear in the exam conducted by the Bihar Combined Entrance Competitive Examination Board.
  2. Take part in counselling
    Choose colleges and branches according to your rank and category.
  3. Report for verification
    Visit the college with original documents after seat allotment.
  4. Complete fee payment
    Fees are paid online through SBI Collect.

Documents to carry

Domicile certificate as per Bihar government norms, plus the other documents listed in your allotment letter.

Eligibility: 10+2. Verify the full document list with the college office.
