About Us

Government Engineering College, Bhojpur, is a government engineering institution established in 2019 by the Government of Bihar under the Department of Science, Technology and Technical Education. Located in Arrah, it has operated from its permanent campus since October 2023 and is affiliated with Bihar Engineering University, Patna.

Vision

To become a recognized centre of excellence in engineering and technical education by providing quality learning, encouraging innovation, promoting research and preparing students to meet emerging technological challenges.

Mission

  1. To provide excellent infrastructure and a supportive academic environment that enhances teaching and learning.
  2. To develop modern laboratories and practical learning facilities in emerging areas of engineering and technology.
  3. To encourage competent, dedicated and research-oriented faculty members who inspire innovation and academic excellence.
  4. To nurture technically skilled, ethical and socially responsible engineers through quality education, practical exposure and continuous learning.

Know Us

About Principal

Dr. Anamika is an academic professional serving as the Principal of Government Engineering College, Bhojpur. With a strong academic background in engineering and research, she brings teaching experience and an academic vision focused on institutional quality and student development. Her specialization includes Power Systems, Renewable Energy, and Price and Demand Forecasting.

Principal's Message

"Dear Students, it gives me immense pleasure to welcome you to Government Engineering College, Bhojpur. Our institution believes that engineering education goes beyond textbooks and examinations. It is about developing practical skills, creative thinking, professional values and the confidence to solve real-world challenges."

GEC Bhojpur offers engineering education across six disciplines with a total sanctioned intake of 360 students, supported by practical learning facilities and a focus on academic excellence.
